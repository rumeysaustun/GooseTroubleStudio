Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short

This site has no accounts, sign-ups or forms; we don't ask for your name, phone number or similar details. When you visit, some technical data is processed by our hosting, security and advertising services. This page explains what that data is and what your rights are.

Who we are

This website (goosetroublestudio.com) is operated by Goose Trouble Studio. For any privacy question or request: admin@goosetroublestudio.com

What data is processed

  • Technical connection data. When you open the site, your IP address and browser and device details are processed by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, to deliver the page and protect the site from attacks.
  • Language preference. If you pick a language from the menu, that choice is stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and is never sent to us. You can remove it by clearing your browser data.
  • Ads. This site shows Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • Fonts. The site's typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser shares your IP address with Google.
  • Email. If you email us, your address and message are used only to reply to you.

We don't use a separate visitor analytics tool, and we don't sell your data.

Ads and cookies

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You can read how Google uses data here.

  • You can turn off personalised ads in Google Ad Settings.
  • You can opt out of other ad vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see Google's consent message before any advertising cookies are used, and can change their choice later from the same message.

Legal basis

We process technical data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and securing the site, and advertising cookies on the basis of your consent where required (Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, KVKK, and the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).

International transfers

Cloudflare and Google may process data on servers outside Türkiye, for example in the United States. These providers operate under their own security measures and contractual commitments.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK and under the GDPR, you have the right to know whether your data is processed, to access it, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, to object to its processing and to withdraw any consent you have given. Send your request to admin@goosetroublestudio.com; we reply within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children of that age.

Our games and Feedzi

This policy covers goosetroublestudio.com only. Our mobile games and Feedzi have their own privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version always lives on this page, with the date at the top.
